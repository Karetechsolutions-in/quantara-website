Services

Two service lines. One partner in India.

After-sales support that keeps your customers happy, and refurbishment that turns returns back into revenue. Use one, or both.

After-sales & support

We look after your customers in India.

Repairs, warranty, customer care and logistics, run as an extension of your own after-sales team.

After-Sales Service

Your brand's service partner in India, handling customer issues from the first complaint to final resolution.

Repairs

Component-level repair of consumer electronics and small appliances, with a documented fault and fix record for every unit.

Warranty Management

Warranty validation, claim processing and repair-or-replace decisions made against your policy, with records you can audit.

Returns & RMA Handling

Return authorisation, intake, diagnosis and routing of each unit, with closed-loop updates to you and your customer.

Customer Care

Support for your customers in India in English, Hindi and Kannada: complaint logging, status updates and service follow-up.

Pan-India Service Logistics

Pickups from customers and warehouses across India, inbound receipt at our facility and return delivery after service.

Returns recovery & refurbishment

We recover value from returned stock.

Inspection, testing, refurbishment and repacking that get returned units back on sale.

Quality Inspection

Visual, cosmetic and functional inspection against your acceptance criteria, with structured defect reporting.

Functional Testing

Standardised bench tests that confirm each unit works against client-defined pass criteria.

Refurbishment & Grading

Cosmetic restoration and A/B/C grading, so every unit goes to the resale channel where it earns the most.

Repacking & Relabeling

Retail-ready or brown-box repacking, barcode and regulatory relabeling with scan-verified workflows.

Secure Data Wiping

Sanitisation of phones, laptops and storage media to recognised standards, with a per-device audit record.

Warehouse & Inventory Support

Inventory audits, put-away, kitting and outbound support for your stock held in India.

Every batch

Reports you can trust from anywhere.

After each consignment you receive a QC and service report with defect summaries, photo evidence and the outcome for every unit: resold, used for warranty, or recycled.
