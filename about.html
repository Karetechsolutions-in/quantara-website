About QUANTARA

Your trusted partner in India.

We help brands and sellers earn revenue from stock they would otherwise lose, and support their customers after every sale.

Who we are

QUANTARA Services Private Limited is a Bengaluru-based partner for global brands and sellers in India. We provide after-sales service, repairs, warranty management, logistics and customer care for consumer electronics and small appliances.

We also refurbish and renew customer returns. A returned product usually means lost revenue. We inspect, repair, refurbish and repack it so it can be sold again, and we explain every step along the way.

We work closely with each client, because our success is measured by how much revenue we help you recover and how much loss we help you avoid.
