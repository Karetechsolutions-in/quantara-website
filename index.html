FAQ

Common questions.

Anything else, write to contact@quantaraservices.in.

We are an overseas brand without an office in India. Can you work with us?

Yes. The whole engagement can be run remotely. We agree scope, SOPs, SLAs and pricing with you over email and video calls, collect and receive your units in India, and share batch reports with photo evidence after every consignment.

Can we talk during our working hours?

Yes. We schedule calls at a time that suits your time zone, including US and European business hours.

What products do you work with?

Consumer electronics, mobile phones and accessories, IT hardware and peripherals, and small appliances. We do not repair large home appliances.

How do you help us recover revenue from returns?

Each returned unit is inspected, repaired where possible, refurbished and graded, then repacked so it can be sold again as new-condition or refurbished stock, or used as a warranty replacement. You get a report showing what was recovered.

What happens to units that cannot be repaired?

They are sent for responsible recycling or disposal, and each one is recorded in the batch report.

Do you handle warranty claims and customer care?

Yes. We validate warranty claims against your policy, manage repairs and replacements, and support your customers in English, Hindi and Kannada.

Do you collect products from across India?

Yes. We arrange pickups from customers and warehouses across India and deliver units back after service.

Do you offer secure data wiping?

Yes. We sanitise storage media following recognised standards and provide a per-device record in the service completion report.

What is a typical turnaround time?

It depends on the service, the condition of the units and the volume. Every engagement has an agreed SLA in the scope of work.

How is pricing structured?

Standard services are quoted per unit. Managed after-sales programs are quoted per project or per dedicated team member. The scope of work is always agreed in writing.

Do you accept small pilot volumes?

Yes. Most engagements start with a pilot batch to agree SOPs, acceptance criteria and report formats.

Where are you located?
