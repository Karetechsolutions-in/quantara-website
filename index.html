FAQ

Common questions.

Everything else is a conversation. Reach us at contact@quantaraservices.in.

What kinds of products do you inspect and refurbish?

We support consumer electronics, IT hardware and accessories, storage devices, healthcare equipment and home & lifestyle products. Workflows are tailored per SKU family and client scope.

Do you offer secure data wiping?

Yes. We sanitize storage media following recognized standards and provide per-device audit records as part of the service completion report.

What is a typical turnaround time?

Turnaround depends on the service scope, unit condition and volume. Every engagement includes an agreed SLA defined in the scope of work.

Can you scale for higher volumes?

Yes. Our capacity model supports ramp-ups through multi-shift operations, pre-agreed staffing and elastic workstations.

How do you report on quality and service?

Every batch is accompanied by a structured QC inspection or service completion report with defect summaries and evidence where applicable.

Do you manage RMA and after-sales support?

Yes. We handle RMA intake, warranty validation, disposition routing and closed-loop communication as an extension of the client's after-sales team.

Where are you located?

Our operations centre is at Plot No-10, Kudregere Road, Near 7 Hills Restaurant, Bangalore 560073, Karnataka, India. Please contact us to arrange a site visit.

How is pricing structured?

Standard services are quoted per-unit; managed operations are quoted on a project or FTE basis. Every quote is transparent, with the scope of work agreed in writing.

How do you ensure product security?

Controlled physical access, unit-level tracking, dual custody for high-value SKUs and end-to-end chain of custody records.

What data protection practices do you follow?

Role-based access, secure transit and rest, and NDAs at both organisational and personnel level.

Can you integrate with our WMS or ERP?

Yes. We support common integration patterns including API, EDI and secure file transfers depending on the client environment.

Do you accept small pilot volumes?

Yes. Most engagements begin with a pilot batch to align on SOPs, acceptance criteria and reporting formats.
